8. From your meals — rules engine vs AI engine

Future Me's "From your meals" card is computed by two engines: the deterministic rules engine (food lexicon + reviewed swap table) and the AI engine (TAMU AI names the student's regulars from their own log text and picks among the same reviewed swaps and today's real menu; every pick is re-verified server-side). Participants see the engine the server's TWIN_ENGINE setting names (…). The rules engine is recorded every day; the AI engine is recorded whenever it runs — every day when TWIN_ENGINE=llm, or on demand with the button below (which spends that participant's own TAMU AI credit). 9. Behavioral twin — diet, activity and sleep

One profile per participant across food, movement and sleep (never combined into a score). The twin asks about patterns it notices (a meal regular, quieter weekdays, irregular bedtimes, a weekday squeeze across domains) and only uses what the participant confirms; steps come from the reviewed library (content/twin_library.json) and the swap table, chosen by readable rules; reasons for "Not for me" / "Didn't fit" become constraints.
