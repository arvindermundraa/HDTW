# Nutrition Team Review Pack

Prepared in response to the 2026-11-06 answers from the nutrition team. Each
file in this folder is one topic the team asked to see. Items inside every
list are numbered so the team can refer to them by number when sending
edits (e.g. "replace item 3 in `01_week1_example_goals.md`").

Anything marked **DRAFT** is copy or data that has not yet been approved by
the nutrition team — please review, edit and return.

## Contents

1. `01_week1_example_goals.md` — every Week 1 example goal by focus area and
   occasion.
2. `02_week2_planning_suggestions.md` — the "what will you choose there"
   actions by focus area and setting, and coping responses by barrier.
3. `03_future_me_knowledge_base.md` — every entry in the Future Me / Coach
   knowledge base, with question, answer and source.
4. `04_swap_suggestions.md` — the current swap list (the "From your meals"
   swaps and the meal-logging swap table).
5. `05_build_a_balanced_meal.md` — the food list with food groups and tags,
   the situations (rounds), and the feedback rules and texts.
6. `06_plan_your_week_ai_examples.md` — 15 example AI responses from TAMU AI,
   paired with the scenario and the student's choice.
7. `07_week2_scenario_feedback.md` — the current DRAFT feedback for the
   restaurant, family-meal (someone else prepares) and at-home scenarios.
8. `08_tamu_dining_data_source.md` — a plain-language explanation of where
   the menu data comes from, what the app reads and stores, and a draft
   permission request the team could send to TAMU Dining.
9. `09_templates.md` — the CSV templates the team will fill in
   (grocery labels, Youth Ambassador video tags, Wings, Week 2 scenario
   scripts). Files live in `templates/`.

A combined Word document (`nutrition_review_pack.docx`) is produced from all
of the above using pandoc.
